# meeting/

Use this folder for anything related to a meeting (planned or completed).

## Suggested files

- `AGENDA.md`: the agenda you’ll send ahead of time
- `NOTES.md`: live notes during the meeting
- `DECISIONS.md`: final decisions + rationale
- `ACTIONS.md`: action items (owner + due date)

## Quick prompts

- What’s the purpose of the meeting?
- What outcomes do we need by the end?
- What decisions must be made (and by whom)?
- What should attendees read or prepare beforehand?
