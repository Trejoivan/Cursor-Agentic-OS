# Set Up work-os

Walk the user through personalizing the workspace. It should feel like a friendly conversation, not a wizard.

## Before You Start

Check whether `GOALS.md` exists and `Active/` already has real categories. If it is already set up, offer to reconfigure. Otherwise, start fresh.

## The Conversation (3 rounds, ~5 minutes)

### Round 1 — Role & Work Types
"Let's get work-os set up for you. What's your role? And what types of work fill your typical day?"

Identify: job title or role, the main categories of work they do.

### Round 2 — Urgency & Pain Points
"What makes something urgent for you? And what do you spend too much time organizing?"

Identify: urgency triggers, biggest pain points, how much structure they actually want.

### Round 3 — Goals
"Last one: what are the main things you're trying to accomplish right now?"

Identify: 2-5 goals, timeframes, success criteria.

## Build the Workspace

Create:

- `GOALS.md` from `GOALS-template.md`, filled in with their goals organized by area
- `BACKLOG.md` from `BACKLOG-template.md` as an empty brain dump inbox
- `Active/` subdirectories based on their role (for example `client-work/`, `projects/`, `meetings/`, `admin/`)
- `Career/accomplishments.md` as an empty starter
- A short user profile note at `Knowledge/profile.md` capturing role, urgency definition, pain points, and goal summary so it can be reread later

If short answers come back, infer sensible defaults from their role rather than asking again.

## Welcome Message

Confirm the categories and goals you created. Suggest natural-language next steps:

- "Process my backlog"
- "What should I work on today?"
- "Add a task: …"

## Rules

- Never show file paths, YAML, frontmatter syntax, or technical details
- Cap the conversation at 3 rounds
- Use plain words — "urgent", "important", "saved for later" — not priority codes
