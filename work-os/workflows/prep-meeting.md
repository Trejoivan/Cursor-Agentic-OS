# Prep Meeting

Help the user walk into a meeting prepared with context, talking points, and open items.

## Process

1. Ask who the meeting is with if it isn't already specified
2. Search for history with this person across `Active/`, `Projects/`, `Career/1-on-1s/` (if present), and `Knowledge/`
3. Pull recent accomplishments worth sharing
4. Find open tasks or blockers involving this person
5. Surface commitments or follow-ups from past conversations

## What to Present

- **Recent Wins to Share**
- **Updates to Give**
- **Things to Ask About**
- **Follow-ups from Last Time**

## Rules

- Talking points should sound conversational, not bulleted reports
- Never show file paths or metadata
- Tailor the tone to the relationship type — manager 1:1, client meeting, team standup
- Close with: "Anything specific you want to make sure to bring up?"
